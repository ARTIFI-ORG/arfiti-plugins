# Procedures Index

Standard operating procedures for accounting and finance operations.

## Available Procedures

### Onboarding
- [Organization Onboarding](onboarding.md) - First-time org setup (MUST use for new organizations)

### Daily Operations
- Daily Cash Position - Morning cash position review

### Periodic Close
- [Month-End Close](month-end-close.md) - Monthly close checklist
- Quarter-End Close - Quarterly close activities
- [Year-End Close](year-end-close.md) - Annual close and audit prep

### Reconciliation
- Bank Reconciliation - Bank account reconciliation

### Tax & Compliance
- Tax returns and information filings follow the entity's jurisdiction. Look up the entity's tax codes with `query(entity_type="tax_code")`, see [Tax Filing](../resources/tax-filing.md), and escalate tax treatment questions to the organisation's tax role (`roles/tax-manager.md` in its generated skill package)

### Support
- [Support Tickets](support.md) - File bug reports, feature requests, check ticket status

### Audit
- Audit Preparation - External audit preparation

## Key Deadlines

### Monthly Close
- Day 1-2: Transaction cutoff
- Day 2-3: Subledger close
- Day 3-4: Adjusting entries
- Day 5: Financial statements

### Quarterly Close
- All monthly close activities
- Plus: Quarter-specific adjustments
- Plus: Management reporting

### Year-End Close
- All quarterly close activities
- Plus: Annual adjustments
- Plus: Audit preparation
- Plus: Tax provisions
