# Estonian VAT Rates

## Current Rates (Effective from July 1, 2025)

| Rate | Application | Effective Since |
|---|---|---|
| **24%** | Standard rate — most goods and services | July 1, 2025 (increased from 22%) |
| **13%** | Reduced rate — accommodation services, with or without breakfast | January 1, 2025 (increased from 9%) |
| **9%** | Reduced rate — press publications in physical or electronic form (except publications mainly made up of advertising or erotic content, which are at the standard rate) | January 1, 2025 (increased from 5%) |
| **9%** | Reduced rate — books, medicines, medical equipment | Long-standing |
| **0%** | Exports outside EU, intra-community B2B supplies | Long-standing |

EMTA lists no VAT rate changes for 2026 ([Maksumuudatused 2026](https://www.emta.ee/uudised/maksumuudatused-2026),
checked 2026-10-31). It says nothing about later years; check before relying on these
rates for periods after 2026.

## Rate History

| Period | Standard | Reduced 1 | Reduced 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| From Jul 2025 | 24% | 13% (accommodation) | 9% (books, medicines, press) |
| Jan-Jun 2025 | 22% | 13% (accommodation) | 9% (books, medicines, press) |
| Jan 2024 - Dec 2024 | 22% | 9% (books, medicines, accommodation) | 5% (press) |
| Before 2024 | 20% | 9% (books, medicines, accommodation) | 5% (press, from 1 Aug 2022; 9% before) |

**Important for transition periods**: When processing invoices near rate change dates,
verify the tax point (supply date) determines the applicable rate, not the invoice date.
Businesses on cash accounting may, until 31 December 2026, keep the old rate for supplies
invoiced and made before 1 January 2025: 9% for accommodation (VAT Act § 46(26), KMD line 2)
and 5% for press publications (§ 46(27), KMD line 2¹). It is a right, not an obligation
([EMTA rates handbook](https://www.emta.ee/node/336/main_chapter/10335/pdf)).
Onboarding creates `EU-EE-RED5-PRESS` (5%) for that press case only; it is transitional.
The 22% and 20% rates have no such transition; they appear on a current return only
through credit invoices for earlier supplies (KMD lines 1² and 1¹).

## Exempt Supplies (No VAT)

The following are exempt from VAT (no input VAT recovery):
- Financial and insurance services
- Health and social services
- Education services
- Postal services (universal service)
- Certain real estate transactions (with option to tax)

## Important Thresholds

| Threshold | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **EUR 40,000** | Annual VAT registration threshold for businesses |
| **EUR 1,000** | Partner reporting threshold for KMD INF annex |
| **EUR 10,000** | Distance selling threshold to other EU states (before OSS applies) |
